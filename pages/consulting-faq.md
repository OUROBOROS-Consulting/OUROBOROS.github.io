## Starting a project

### What happens on the free 20-minute AI privacy call?

We will outline where your team's AI data goes, identify the main question or risk to investigate, and agree on a useful next step. You do not need to share confidential details. There is no obligation to hire me.

### What should I send through the inquiry form?

Only a high-level description of the problem and a way to reach you. Formspree processes the form and forwards it by email. Do not send passwords, secrets, client records, or other confidential material. The form is not legally privileged and does not create a client relationship.

### What if I am not sure what service I need?

Choose the closest option or select “Other / not sure.” The first conversation can help establish whether the work fits and what to do next. If it is outside my scope, I will say so.

## Scope and limits

### What does the firm do?

Private AI systems and agentic workflows, AI privacy and governance reviews, data-quality audits, and custom tools for sensitive information. Web and graphic design, plus AI privacy education and training, are also available as separate services. [See the design services](/services/design/).

### Does “private AI” mean zero risk?

No. Local hosting can reduce exposure to outside providers, but it does not remove risks from access, connected services, configuration, or human decisions. I explain the data path and safeguards, and I do not promise perfect security.

### Do you provide cybersecurity audits or legal advice?

No. AI privacy and governance work is practical consulting, not a penetration test, security certification, or legal advice. I will recommend a qualified specialist when the work calls for one.
