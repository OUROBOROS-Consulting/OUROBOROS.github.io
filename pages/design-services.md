Web and graphic design are secondary services at ouroborOS Consulting. The firm's
priority is private AI and data integrity. When the problem is how your brand or
digital experience communicates, I can help make it clearer and easier to use.

## What I design

- **Visual identity and graphic design:** A coherent visual direction and the
  graphic assets your project needs.
- **Websites and digital interfaces:** Page structure, user flows, and interface
  designs that help people find information and take action.
- **Prototypes and design systems:** Figma prototypes and reusable visual
  patterns to make the design easier to review and hand off.
- **Accessibility-aware experiences:** Clear hierarchy, readable content, and
  interface decisions that account for different user needs.

Design is created by me in Figma, not generated by AI. Scope, deliverables, and
price are agreed for each project.

## How a project starts

We begin with the audience, the problem, and what the design needs to make
possible. Then we agree on the work and deliverables before design begins. The
intake form lets you ask about graphic design, a website, or a digital
experience; you do not need to send confidential materials.

## See the work

The [design portfolio](/design-portfolio/) includes an in-house brand and
website case study. It documents the process; it is not a client engagement or
a claim of measured client outcomes.

[Send a design inquiry](/intake/). Choose **Design** and describe the project
