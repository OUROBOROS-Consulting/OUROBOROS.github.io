    body_paragraphs:

      - "**Check the route:** Review the available product and hosting details. Do not assume a vendor's privacy claims cover your use case."

    heading: "AI Governance That People Can Use"
    body_paragraphs:
      - "Set rules your team can follow under real working conditions."
      - "**Access and approval:** Decide which data AI can use and when a person must approve an action."
      - "**Human review:** Define who checks outputs before they affect customers, operations, or public reports."
      - "**Plain-language policy:** Give people clear directions on what not to paste, what tools are approved, and who to ask."

      - "**Next step:** Get a short list of options and a clear recommendation, including when not to use AI."
