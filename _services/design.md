sections:
  - id: philosophy
    heading: Design Philosophy
    body_paragraphs:
      - >
        **Less as a principle, not a constraint.** Good design removes friction and clarifies intent. 
        My work is built on the belief that thoughtful choices—about what to emphasize, what to omit, 
        and how to structure information—create lasting value.
      - >
        **Accessibility as a design requirement, not an add-on.** Every interface I build is designed 
        with WCAG standards in mind from the start. Clear typography, sufficient contrast, semantic markup, 
        and keyboard navigation are not afterthoughts; they are foundational.
      - >
        **Systems over one-offs.** Whether a single landing page or a full brand system, I build for 
        longevity and scalability. Reusable patterns, documented tokens, and maintainable structures 
        mean your design can evolve without constant rework.
      - >
        **Substance in form.** Visual identity should reflect the work it represents. My clients span 
        consulting, research, and professional services. The design communicates credibility, precision, 
        and human judgment—not decorative flair.

  - id: process
    heading: My Process
    body_paragraphs:
      - >
        **Discovery:** I begin with conversations and briefs to understand the work, audience, and 
        constraints. For new clients, this may include an initial paid session to align on scope, 
        timeline, and expectations.
      - >
        **Research & Strategy:** I analyze competitive contexts, audit existing materials, and identify 
        opportunities. For UI/UX work, this includes user research and information architecture.
      - >
        **Concept & Direction:** I develop initial concepts, visual directions, and structural approaches. 
        You review and provide feedback before moving into refinement.
      - >
        **Development & Refinement:** I build the design system or interface, create detailed mockups, 
        and refine based on feedback. Accessibility testing and cross-browser/device review are integrated.
      - >
        **Handoff & Support:** I deliver production-ready assets, documentation, and guidance for 
        implementation. Ongoing support is available for adjustments and future iterations.

  - id: values
    heading: Core Values
    body_paragraphs:
      - >
        **Innovation:** I embrace new tools and approaches that improve the design process without 
        sacrificing quality. AI, automation, and emerging technologies are part of my toolkit when they 
        serve the work.
      - >
        **Reason:** Design decisions should be grounded in evidence and clear thinking. I prioritize 
        understanding the problem before proposing solutions.
      - >
        **Efficiency:** Wasted effort compounds. I document my work, create reusable patterns, and focus 
        human attention where it belongs—on difficult choices and creative work.
      - >
        **Autonomy:** I design systems that respect user agency. Interfaces should support informed decision-making, 
        not obscure it.
      - >
        **Integrity:** I put quality and ethics first. When a request conflicts with accessibility, 
        usability, or the work's integrity, I push back.
      - >
        **Transparency:** I document my decisions and explain the reasoning. You should understand 
        why a design choice was made, not just what it is.
      - >
        **Security:** I design with data privacy in mind. Interfaces should not unnecessarily expose 
        sensitive information, and design systems should support secure implementation.
