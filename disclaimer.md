A firm that serves people who have been failed by institutions owes them clarity about its own limits. Here are mine.

## This is not legal advice

I am not a lawyer, and this firm does not practice law. Nothing on this site, and nothing in any engagement, is legal advice. I hold a hard line against the unauthorized practice of law. That line does not move.

This is a feature, not a shortfall. Legal questions deserve a licensed attorney who answers for their advice and carries the duty that comes with it. When your situation needs one, I will say so plainly and refer you out. Knowing the boundary is part of the service.

## This is not investigative work for hire

I am not a licensed private detective. I do not investigate people on a client's behalf, and I do not accept engagements aimed at a third party. The research work described on this site is one of two things: helping you obtain and organize records that are yours to ask for, or teaching you to do the searching yourself.

That boundary is regulatory and it is ethical. The same techniques that let a journalist verify a source let an abuser find someone who moved. Every engagement is screened for that, and I decline work whose purpose I cannot establish. If you need a licensed investigator, you need someone other than me, and I will say so on the first call.

## This is not mental-health or crisis care

I am not a therapist, a clinician, or a crisis counselor. The writing and resources here are educational and informational. They are meant to help you understand what happened to you and orient your next decision. They are not treatment, diagnosis, or a substitute for care from a qualified professional.

If you are in crisis or in danger right now, contact emergency services or a crisis line. The [survival guide](/survival-guide.html) lists resources that can help.

## Mention is not endorsement

This site names people, organizations, institutions, tools, and research. A mention is a reference, nothing more. It does not imply that the named party endorses this firm, my work, or my conclusions. When someone has endorsed the work, I say so directly.
