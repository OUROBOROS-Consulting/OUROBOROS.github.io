Why This Work

Leaks and bad numbers start with choices. Make the choices visible.

During the COVID-19 response at the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, I shipped forecasting and anomaly-detection models that flagged impossible numbers across 6,000+ hospitals before CDC leadership reported them to Congress, the media, and the public.

I also led the move of HHS Protect Public and Vaccines.gov to CDC and ASPR: 3,900+ users in under six months, with zero disruption. The HHS CIO recognized my work with two separate HHS awards.

Today, I build locally hosted AI and agentic workflows that keep confidential data inside your walls. I audit data so reported numbers hold up, and build tools that make complex information usable.

My research in Bayesian statistics is peer-reviewed. I write about agentic AI and privacy at Problematic by Design. Every engagement is confidential, with plain-language reporting.

If your data can't afford to leak or be wrong, request a free 20-minute AI privacy call. I'll follow up within 72 hours.
