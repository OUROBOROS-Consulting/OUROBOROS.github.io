## Research Focus

- Bayesian methods, hierarchical models, prior specification in clinical and forensic contexts
- Personality psychopathology (HiTOP, RDoC), trans-diagnostic measurement
- Brain imaging and correlated data, mixed-effects modeling
- Algorithmic fairness, bias in classification systems, implications for institutionally vulnerable populations
- Statistical forensics, evidentiary standards, expert-witness methodology

## Design Philosophy

- **Less as a principle, not a constraint.** Good design removes friction and clarifies intent
- **Accessibility as a design requirement, not an add-on.** WCAG standards from the start
- **Systems over one-offs.** Build for longevity and scalability
- **Substance in form.** Visual identity reflects the work it represents

## Core Values

- **Innovation:** New tools that improve quality without sacrificing it
- **Reason:** Evidence-grounded decisions, understanding the problem before proposing solutions
- **Efficiency:** Document work, create reusable patterns, focus human attention on difficult choices
- **Autonomy:** Systems that respect user agency, support informed decision-making
- **Integrity:** Quality and ethics first, push back when requests conflict with accessibility or usability
- **Transparency:** Document decisions, explain reasoning
- **Security:** Data privacy in mind, interfaces that do not unnecessarily expose sensitive information

- **Custom Tools for Complex Information:** Purpose-built tools that fit your environment, maintainable systems

## Philosophy

> Systems are not neutral. Statistical choices embed values. The question is not whether your model has assumptions — it does — but whether you have named them.

> Generative AI optimizes for appearance. Professionals optimize for accountability. Design is about carving a unique psychological space, not just predicting the next most likely pixel.
