# Accessibility Statement

OUROBOROS Consulting is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We are continually improving the user experience for everyone and applying the relevant accessibility standards to ensure our website is accessible to all.

## Our Commitment

We strive to make our website accessible to everyone, including people with visual, auditory, cognitive, neurological, and motor disabilities. We believe that our digital products should be usable by everyone, regardless of their abilities or circumstances.

## Standards Conformance

Our website conformance goal is **Level AA** of Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2. We strive to meet the following criteria:

- **Text Alternatives**: Text alternatives are provided for non-text content so that information conveys the same information or function to everyone.
- **Time-based Media**: Alternatives for time-based media are provided to ensure information is conveyed to everyone.
- **Accessible Parsing**: Content can be parsed without ambiguity so that it can be presented in different ways, including for users of assistive technologies.
- **Input Modalities**: Functions are operable by a variety of input modalities, including keyboard, voice, and touch.
- **Multiple Ways to Access Content**: Information and methods can be presented in ways that are understandable to users with different needs.
- **No Unexpected Content**: Web pages do not cause seizures or physical reactions.
- **Input in a Variety of Ways**: Input can be provided by a variety of modalities, including keyboard, voice, and touch.
- **Clear Contrast**: Visual presentation provides a high level of color contrast for readability.
- **No Content Due to Color**: Color is not the only way information is conveyed.
- **Focus Visible**: Keyboard focus indicators are clear and visible.
- **Keyboard Access**: All functionality is available from a keyboard.
- **Navigable**: Page navigation and structure are understandable and predictable.
- **Input Assistance**: Users are helped when they make input errors.
- **Compatible**: Compatible with current and emerging assistive technologies.

## Testing and Certification

Our website undergoes regular accessibility testing and evaluation:

- **Automated Testing**: We use automated tools to identify and fix common accessibility issues.
- **Manual Testing**: Our team performs manual testing to ensure the website is accessible.
- **User Testing**: We conduct user testing with people who have disabilities to identify and address barriers.

## Known Limitations

While we strive for full accessibility, some limitations may exist:

- **Third-party Content**: Some third-party content may not be fully accessible. We are working to identify and address these issues.
- **Legacy Content**: Some legacy content may not meet current accessibility standards. We are working to update this content.
- **Emerging Technology**: Some emerging technologies may not be fully supported. We are working to improve compatibility.

## How to Request Accessibility Accommodations

If you are unable to access any aspect of our website, or experience any difficulty in using any functions of our website, please contact us at **accessibility@ouroboros.com**. We will work with you to resolve the issue as quickly as possible.

### Alternative Formats

If you need our website content or services in an alternative format (such as large print, Braille, audio description, or accessible electronic format), please contact us at **accessibility@ouroboros.com**. We will work with you to provide the requested format.

### Assistive Technology Support

If you use assistive technology and experience any issues with our website, please contact us at **accessibility@ouroboros.com**. We will work with you to identify and address any barriers.

## Accessibility Feedback

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of our website. If you have any questions, suggestions, or concerns, please contact us at **accessibility@ouroboros.com**. We will respond to all feedback within 30 business days.

### Accessibility Report

You can submit an accessibility report by emailing **accessibility@ouroboros.com** with the following information:

- Your name (optional)
- Your contact information (optional)
- Description of the issue
- Steps to reproduce the issue
- Browser and device information
- Screenshots (if applicable)
